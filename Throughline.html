About

I started out as a data engineer. It's the kind of work where nobody notices you unless the pipeline breaks: millions of records a day, cleaned and validated until they're something worth trusting. That instinct never really left me. I still won't ship a model on data I haven't looked at myself.

Somewhere along the way, the question stopped being how do we move the data and turned into what can we get it to understand. I followed that into machine learning, then deep learning, then into building the AI systems that actually reason over data in real time: RAG pipelines, multi-agent workflows, models running at the edge instead of waiting on a round trip to the cloud.

I'm a data person and an AI person, in that order. The engineering instinct came first, and it's still the one I trust most. Give me a real system to put my hands on and I'll take it over a slide about one, any day.
